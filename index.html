Ranking Shorts

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel PYRO (@pyro9039).

Ranking Shorts runs on its owner's own computer. It assembles short, ranking-style comedy videos ("Ranking the funniest ...") and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

What it does

  1. Picks a video topic and gathers short public clips for it.
  2. Edits the clips into a vertical Short with a title, a ranking list and on-screen labels, and credits every original creator in the video description.
  3. Uploads the finished Short to the owner's channel and schedules its publish time.
  4. Reads the view counts of the channel's own uploads, so future topics can follow what viewers enjoy.

Google and YouTube access

The tool signs in with Google OAuth and asks for only two permissions on the owner's account:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish the Shorts it creates.
  • View YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read view counts of its own uploads.
